🔒 Confidentiality Promise

Every successful sourcing relationship is built on trust. We treat every client's supply chain, pricing strategy and manufacturing network as strictly confidential.

Over the last 13+ years, we have worked with emerging startups, rapidly growing D2C brands, large importers, national distributors and well-known consumer brands across multiple industries.

Many of our partners compete directly in the same market. Protecting their sourcing network is one of the reasons they continue working with us.

Our experience covers OEM manufacturing, ODM development, supplier sourcing, factory negotiations, quality control and complete supply chain management.

Several products sourced through our network are now sold across Amazon, Flipkart, modern retail chains and international marketplaces.

Whether you are launching your first product or scaling an established business, your supplier information, pricing strategy and manufacturing details remain protected with LISHTO.

Due to signed NDAs and long-term business agreements, we do not publicly disclose client names or supplier information. References can be shared only during genuine business discussions where confidentiality permits.
