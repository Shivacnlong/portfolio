🔒 Confidentiality Promise

Every successful sourcing relationship is built on trust. We treat every client's supply chain, pricing strategy and manufacturing network as strictly confidential.

Over the last 13+ years, we have worked with emerging startups, rapidly growing D2C brands, large importers, national distributors and well-known consumer brands across multiple industries.

Some of our collaborations include businesses featured on Shark Tank India, fast-growing Mumbai-based brands, category-leading grooming brands, high-volume e-commerce sellers and companies with nationwide retail presence.

Many of our partners compete directly in the same market. Protecting their sourcing network is one of the reasons they continue working with us.

Our experience covers product sourcing, OEM manufacturing, ODM development, factory negotiations, quality control and complete supply chain management.

Several products sourced through our network are now sold across Amazon, Flipkart, modern retail chains and international marketplaces.

We regularly work with factories manufacturing millions of units annually for global brands.

Whether you are launching your first product or scaling an established business, our focus remains the same—quality, transparency and long-term partnerships.

Your product idea, supplier information, pricing and manufacturing details remain confidential with us at every stage.

Confidentiality is not just a policy—it is one of the core reasons our clients choose to work with LISHTO.

Due to signed NDAs and long-term business agreements, we do not publicly disclose the brands or companies we have worked with on this website. However, during a genuine business discussion, we are happy to share relevant client references, project experience and industry background where confidentiality permits.
