China Doesn't Build Businesses. Sales Do.

When I was in China, I realized something that completely changed my perspective.

Everyone thinks factories create successful companies. They don't.

Factories only manufacture products.

Customers don't buy because a product came from China.

Customers buy because someone knows how to sell, market, position, and build trust.

China gives you manufacturing power.

Sales give you business power.

I visited factories with world-class machinery producing millions of products every month. Yet many of those factories had almost no brand of their own.

On the other hand, many global brands never manufacture anything themselves. They focus on branding, marketing, customer experience, and distribution.

That was my biggest lesson.

If you can combine great manufacturing with great sales, you create an unfair advantage.

That's exactly what I believe modern entrepreneurs should build.

Don't spend years searching for the perfect product.

Learn how to create demand.

Products can be copied.

Sales cannot.

That mindset changed the way I look at business forever.
