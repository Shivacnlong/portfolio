China Doesn't Build Businesses. Sales Do.

27 June 2026

China Didn’t Make Me Money. Sales Did.

When I was in China, most people thought my focus was factories, products, exhibitions, or supplier meetings.

But the truth is different.

60% of my focus was on selling.

While walking through markets in Shenzhen, sitting in factories in Dongguan, or traveling across China, my mind was still in India.

Calling customers.
Replying to inquiries.
Negotiating deals.
Following up on payments.
Closing orders.

Because factories don’t pay you.
Products don’t pay you.
Supplier visits don’t pay you.
Customers pay you.

Many entrepreneurs spend months searching for the perfect product, perfect supplier, perfect packaging, and perfect logo.

But they forget the most important thing.

Someone has to buy it.

If sales stop, everything stops.

No matter where you are in the world, your business grows when you stay close to customers.

Factories create products.

Sales create businesses.
