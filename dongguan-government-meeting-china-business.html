Inside Dongguan: What I Learned Meeting China's Business Power Players

By Shiva Dongguan, China

A recent trip to Dongguan gave me a front-row seat to something most outsiders never get to see: how China's regional business elite actually operate. I had the opportunity to sit down with several high-ranking government-connected officials — true powerhouses of the region, each with a hand in multiple brands, companies, and industries. Spending time with them was a masterclass in business acumen built over decades.

Over the course of these meetings, we moved well beyond small talk. I recorded several conversations covering how local government actually operates behind the scenes in China, how investment deals are structured, and — perhaps most importantly — how business relationships are built and maintained in a culture where trust is the real currency. I'll be sharing that footage soon, and I believe it will be genuinely useful for anyone looking to do business in China.

What We Actually Talked About

Beyond the formalities, the discussions covered ground that most foreign entrepreneurs struggle to access on their own:

  • How to find real investors in China — and how to tell serious capital from empty promises.
  • How to find reliable suppliers — the vetting process that separates long-term manufacturing partners from one-time vendors.
  • How to start a company in China as a foreigner — the practical, on-the-ground steps that don't show up in generic guides.
It felt less like a business dinner and more like recording a podcast — packed with practical knowledge and real-world experience you simply can't get from a Google search.

What stood out most was how consistently relationships came before contracts. Every deal, every introduction, every opportunity in that room traced back to trust that had been built over years — not weeks. It's a reminder that in China, the paperwork is often the last step, not the first.

Stay tuned for the video series — there's a lot more to share.
